Privacy Policy for Glob FM

This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you use the Glob FM application.

Data Collection and Usage

Glob FM does not collect, store, or share any personal user data. The app is designed to function locally on your device for streaming radio stations and podcasts.

Permissions

The application requires the following permissions to function:

  • Internet Access: Necessary to stream radio and podcast audio from the internet.

Third-Party Services

This app connects to third-party radio stations and podcast providers. We do not have control over how these third-party services collect or handle your data when you access their streams.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

Contact Us
