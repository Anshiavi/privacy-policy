Privacy Policy for POS Pro

Last updated: August 6, 2026

At POS Pro, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard the data you provide when using our Point of Sale application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide you with a functional and reliable Point of Sale experience. This includes:

  • Transaction Data: Details regarding sales, items sold, prices, and timestamps.
  • User/Employee Information: If you use user management features, we may store names, email addresses, or roles for login and access control purposes.
  • Usage Information: Basic app usage data (such as app version, device type) to help us improve performance and stability.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To operate and maintain the Point of Sale system.
  • To process transactions and generate receipts.
  • To manage user accounts and roles.
  • To improve our application based on usage trends.

3. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. Please note that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

4. Third-Party Services

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may use third-party services (like analytics tools) solely to help us understand how the app is performing, and they are obligated to protect your data.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding your data, such as accessing, correcting, or deleting the information we hold.

6. Account and Data Deletion

Because POS Pro operates locally on your device, all application data (including transactions and user information) is stored directly on your device's local storage. We do not have access to or control over this data.

If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, you can do so by taking the following actions on your device:

  • Clear App Data: Go to your device's Settings > Apps > POS Pro > Storage and select "Clear Data" or "Clear Storage".
  • Uninstall the App: Deleting the application from your device will also remove all local data associated with it.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@pospro.xyz.
